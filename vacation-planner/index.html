Build your own yearly vacation planner

  1. Start with the dates you cannot move

    Check your school’s published term dates and your work calendar. Create categories for school breaks and work commitments, then mark those ranges in YearMap. Add travel days or fixed family events that could affect a trip.

  2. Give each possible trip its own color

    Drag across the dates you are considering and assign them to a category. Use separate categories for two alternatives so they remain easy to compare. YearMap supports up to five color categories.

  3. Read the overlaps and keep the details close

    Look for work commitments inside a proposed trip, or trips extending beyond a school break. Add range notes for questions such as “leave approval pending” or “allow a day to get home.” Category totals count recorded calendar days; check your employer’s leave rules separately.

  4. Agree on a window before making bookings

    Discuss the options with the people involved and confirm the dates against their schedules. Once you choose, edit or remove the unused alternative in your own calendar.

YearMap is a year-at-a-glance calendar for Chrome. You enter and compare plans yourself; it does not book trips, approve leave, or automatically calculate the best PTO dates.

Share the big picture, then export your chosen dates

Send a read-only YearMap snapshot so someone else can see your proposed trip alongside the rest of the year. They can open the link on a phone or computer, but cannot edit it. A shared snapshot does not update when you change your calendar; send a new link after revising the plan.

Anyone with a shared link can view its included content. Review what you share, especially notes and work details. Read more about YearMap’s privacy practices.

When your dates are settled, export ranges as an ICS file for Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or another calendar app. Export is a file download, not a live connection between calendars. See the support page if you need help.

Vacation planning questions

Can I edit the example or use it as a template?

The example is read-only. Install YearMap and create your own categories and dates; the example does not copy into your extension automatically.

Are school holidays filled in for me?

This guide’s example uses fictional school breaks. Enter your own school’s published dates and check them before booking. They are not inferred from this example.

Can my family edit the same calendar?

Shared links are read-only snapshots. They let others review the plan, but do not provide collaborative editing or automatically update after changes.

Can I make my vacation plan on my phone?

Creating and editing plans requires the YearMap extension in Chrome on a computer. Read-only shared calendars can be viewed in a phone’s browser.

Is YearMap free?

Yes. YearMap is free, requires no account, and supports up to five color categories. It opens in your new tab while enabled and can also be opened from the extension icon.
